01 / WELCOME TO LANEWAY MELBOURNE

A little outside
the ordinary.

Laneway's Melbourne campus is on Fitzroy Street in St Kilda, a neighbourhood known for its independent spirit, waterfront setting and mix of cultures.

It is a place to slow down enough to think differently, then step out into a city that rewards curiosity. Bring your own perspective. Find new ways forward.
